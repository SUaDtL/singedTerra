These are unfinished-build playtests; owner acceptance is pending.

Choose **Launcher** or **Repair**, then **Deploy**. Combat earns **3 Scrap per kill**; after two kills you can afford a first upgrade. Open **Mechanics Shop**, choose Attack/Defense/Utility and buy a compatible upgrade. Opening the shop keeps combat running; **Pause** gives you time to read and buy. For a fitting-specific comparison, use **Launcher Attack** with Launcher or **Field Repair** with Repair. Close the shop to watch the effect. After ordinary defeat, manually **Claim** the one Salvage, enter the permanent workshop and invest it. A later deployment retains permanent levels and starts with zero temporary levels and Scrap.

The prototype has a **180-second** limit. Reaching that limit, unfinished exit/reload or technical failure earns **no Salvage**. Ordinary defeat earns one: the resulting **fast-loss** incentive means losing quickly can advance permanent upgrades faster than a strong surviving build. Temporary Scrap never converts into Salvage. Keep your current run open if you want to finish it; reloading discards its temporary progress.

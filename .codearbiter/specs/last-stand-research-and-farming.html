CR-QUEUE

Two slots, bounded queues and retained switching progress

must
Applicability
Modeunconditional
RationaleRequired for this proposed research and farming sprint.
Constraint refs
  • CON-REVIEW
  • CON-PRESERVE
  • CON-EVIDENCE
Guarantees
  • At most two slots and four funded projects exist in this sprint. Charge shared Salvage at start/queue/resume. No automatic repeat, automatic rank purchase or spending after an offline interval.
  • A track cannot be funded in two slots. The next rank may queue behind its active prerequisite only in that same slot; all other queued work must already meet its prerequisites. Reject duplicate project/rank intents.
  • Resolve offline finishes chronologically. At a shared finish timestamp commit all completions before starting any queues, so a newly completed Research Speed rank applies consistently. Process only the already funded projects.
  • Lock a new rank duration when it starts, using eligible research bonuses then. A queued rank does not progress before its slot is available. Unslotted partial projects do not progress.
  • Switching suspends the unfinished active rank, saves elapsed progress and its original charged cost/duration, and refunds that charge once. Resuming or queuing that partial rank repays the stored cost and retains its locked duration; no repricing or clock reset exploit.
  • Keep at most one partial next-rank record per catalog track. Completion consumes it. A resumed project can finish only while funded in a slot. Reconcile due completions before stale switch/cancel actions.
  • Removing queued work refunds its charge; a resumed partial project retains previous progress. A Switch intent atomically suspends/refunds the old active project and funds/starts the selected replacement. A still-valid queue stays queued; an invalidated dependent queue is refunded and cleared.
  • Check the complete Switch intent, including available balance plus refunds and the selected replacement cost, before mutation. An unaffordable or stale replacement leaves active project, queue, partial state and wallet unchanged. A plain Suspend without a replacement instead promotes a valid existing queue immediately, or refunds/clears it if its prerequisite was removed.
  • A write/readback failure, stale intent or second tab cannot duplicate elapsed progress, charges, refunds, completions or slot starts.

CR-TRACKS

Four complementary research tracks

must
Applicability
Modeunconditional
RationaleRequired for this proposed research and farming sprint.
Constraint refs
  • CON-REVIEW
  • CON-PRESERVE
  • CON-EVIDENCE
Guarantees
  • Game Speed has eight ranks, each adding 0.5 to the earned maximum from 1x through 5x. It affects combat, never the research clock.
  • Proposed supporting catalogs: 100 ranks each, adding 0.02 per rank to the relevant multiplier (1 + 0.02*r), with independent staged costs and durations. These are calibration seeds, not confirmed final balance.
  • Salvage Recovery multiplies repeatable wave Salvage only. It never multiplies one-time milestone rewards. Persist fractional carry in profile state and apply new completed ranks once at future wave transactions.
  • Scrap Logistics multiplies kill Scrap income, after the run economy and Scrap Yield upgrade effect. Its fractional remainder belongs to the run checkpoint: unfinished-wave rewards and carry roll back together. Apply new completed ranks only to subsequent reward events, never re-award past kills.
  • Research Speed divides a newly started project duration by (1 + 0.02*r), except its own track, which always uses its base duration. Already-started or suspended rank durations remain locked; queued new ranks use bonuses at their actual start time.
  • Use checked fixed-point/rational reward arithmetic and durable remainders; no repeated floor loss or overflow. Benefits must affect actual rewards/timing, not just descriptive labels.
  • Typed stable IDs define prerequisites, caps, prices and base durations. Keep equipment, Skills, monetization and additional resource currencies outside this catalog.

CR-SAVE

Atomic migration across shared funding, research and income

must
Applicability
Modeunconditional
RationaleRequired for this proposed research and farming sprint.
Constraint refs
  • CON-REVIEW
  • CON-PRESERVE
  • CON-EVIDENCE
Guarantees
  • Preserve all v1-v4 wallets, permanent levels, tier records, fittings, first clears, pending legacy Claim, preferences and active run state. Confirm write/readback and retain recoverable original data on failure.
  • Research shares transaction ownership with Salvage spending and rewards, while its clock/catalog remain typed modules. A combat checkpoint rollback never undoes a separately committed profile research completion or Shop purchase.
  • A mid-wave profile save retains the last completed-wave combat snapshot. Active kill Scrap, temporary purchases and run fractional carry must not leak into a completed checkpoint through a research transaction.
  • Persist slot entitlements, active/queued funding, completion identities, bounded suspended-rank progress, locked durations/costs and fractional carries. No extra research wallet exists.
  • Migrate the seven legacy upgrade entries by stable identity; append Scrap Yield at zero without shifting old levels or fabricating history. Legacy active runs keep their original track roster, prices, paid totals, effects and base rewards; new runs include the new track.
  • All new permanent purchases use current profile pricing even with a legacy run suspended. Active runs retain frozen permanent levels. New profile research income modifiers apply prospectively to legacy reward events, with legacy base rewards unchanged.
  • Version earned-Scrap accounting as well as prices. Legacy validation currently assumes EarnedScrap equals UniqueKills times 3; migrate its accepted base total, then record future base/bonus earnings and fractional carry with bounded checked counters. Never apply the latest research multiplier to historical kills. Preserve total earned = spent + available and checkpoint all related counters together.
  • Use one browser writer for the entire envelope and stale-version guards. Unknown versions, malformed projects, illegal duplicates, invalid caps, unsafe numeric values or corrupt state fail visibly without reset.
  • Retry commits the pending transaction once; queued/refunded funds cannot be replayed. After release, repair new-version saves forward rather than deploying a parser that destroys them.

Bulk upgrades for long farming runs

must
Applicability

Guarantees

SEC-ECONOMY

Source audit: current Last Stand economy

Blocks

The current run price is 5*n plus a quadratic term after purchase 8, and effects also grow nonlinearly. The complaint is therefore not literally a purely linear formula: costs, benefits and earning rates are mismatched.

Cannon Cycle permanent cap costs 94 Salvage in total, reachable from a fresh first clear through Tier 1 wave 52 if unspent. Existing farm cap checks cover Cannon Attack but miss that short utility track.

At zero permanent Cannon Attack and level 500, the next rank costs 8581 Scrap and raises damage from 53432 to 53639 (about 0.387%). Current maximum income is 20 kills times 3 Scrap = 60 per wave, so that rank costs at least 144 waves before any other spending. This is source arithmetic, not proof that a fresh profile can survive to that level.

The corrective loop is income investment plus per-track cost/effect curves, useful permanent starting levels, shared research opportunity cost and mixed completion horizons. Increasing caps or adding bulk controls cannot alone repair that loop.

Legacy restore currently recomputes spent Scrap from catalog prices and requires earned Scrap to equal kills times 3. Pin old prices/roster/effects and migrate accepted base earnings; then persist prospective base/bonus earnings and fractional carry. Current permanent purchases use current prices. Never reinterpret historical kills with a later research multiplier.

SEC-CLOCK

Timing, switching and shared storage

Blocks

Research uses real time outside deterministic combat. Reconcile both active slots and funded queues chronologically; complete all projects at a common timestamp before starting queues. Only prepaid projects progress while closed; there is no automatic purchase or unbounded catch-up loop.

Switching preserves elapsed time and original cost/duration, refunds once, and requires repayment to resume. A selected replacement takes the active slot while a valid queue remains queued; an invalid dependent queue is refunded. An unaffordable switch changes nothing. Plain Suspend promotes a valid queue. Partial records are bounded to one unfinished rank per track, and one track cannot occupy multiple funded slots.

Profile research and shared Salvage transactions retain the last completed-wave combat snapshot. Run Scrap and fractional kill carry roll back with unfinished waves; separately committed research never rolls back with combat. A single writer owns the entire envelope.

Device time supports offline elapsed intervals, not secure server authority. Monotonic open-session timing and validated UTC anchors handle rollback and finite arithmetic. No backend, cloud save or offline combat is added.

CR-WAVES

Finite waves and continuing runs

must
Applicability
Modeunconditional
RationalePart of the proposed complete wave progression sprint.
Constraint refs
  • CON-REVIEW
  • CON-PRESERVE
  • CON-EVIDENCE
Guarantees
  • A wave has a finite authored roster. It clears only after every planned enemy has spawned and been defeated; an empty gap between spawns cannot clear it.
  • A new wave cannot overlap the previous cohort. A short automatic intermission begins only after checkpoint confirmation and obeys pause/speed. Set its duration from the full-run pacing budget, including cumulative downtime across thousands of waves.
  • Keep deterministic fixed-step simulation. Replace the player-facing time horizon with continuing waves; diagnostic ceilings must not grant rewards or erase a resumable run.
  • Bound simultaneous enemies, runtime allocations, numeric growth and retained state. Prove continuation and checkpoint recovery at 300, 1000, 2000, 5000 and beyond 5000 waves without an ever-growing wave roster or reward history; support the owner multi-hour operational horizon.

CR-REWARDS

Clear rewards that cannot duplicate or disappear

must
Applicability
Modeunconditional
RationalePart of the proposed complete wave progression sprint.
Constraint refs
  • CON-REVIEW
  • CON-PRESERVE
  • CON-EVIDENCE
Guarantees
  • Commit cleared-wave rewards, tier unlocks, first-clear milestone ledger and next-wave checkpoint as one durable record before advancing.
  • Keep Scrap for in-run upgrades and Salvage for permanent upgrades. Author repeatable earnings and first-clear bonuses for hundreds/thousands of waves, balanced together with upgrade costs/effects/caps; the prior fixed one-Salvage-per-five-waves schedule is withdrawn.
  • First-clear bonuses/unlocks are once per tier/milestone across runs; repeatable payouts are once per run/wave. No replay, retry or reload double-awards.
  • For new wave runs, bank awards automatically at clear and show a compact receipt; defeat/abandon adds no separate payout. Preserve an existing legacy pending Claim exactly once during migration.
  • If the last enemy and the tank die in the same simulation step, bank the completed-wave reward and finalize the run atomically; never create a living resume checkpoint with zero hull.
  • Extend the existing upgrade economy beyond prototype depth without erasing current levels or investment. Attack/health growth and bounded utility tracks must stay meaningful and numerically valid through mature 2000-plus-wave runs; no zero/negative firing intervals, overflow, mandatory purchase prompts or immediate full-economy saturation.

CR-CHECKPOINT

Full run checkpoint and deterministic restore

must
Applicability
Modeunconditional
RationalePart of the proposed complete wave progression sprint.
Constraint refs
  • CON-REVIEW
  • CON-PRESERVE
  • CON-EVIDENCE
Guarantees
  • Confirm a starting checkpoint before wave 1. At each clear, snapshot hull/max hull, temporary upgrades and their cost effects, Scrap, run/tier IDs, fitting, deployment stat baseline, wave/rules version, simulation clock/cooldowns and generator/seed state needed for deterministic continuation.
  • A reload resumes at the start of the unfinished wave with the exact latest committed checkpoint. Mid-wave purchases and spending roll back together; completed rewards stay banked.
  • Resume waits for explicit player input and starts paused; do not simulate time while the tab was closed or replay historical effects/audio.
  • The same input sequence after restore yields the same combat state as an uninterrupted checkpoint continuation.

SEC-SMARTS

SMARTS design rationale

Blocks

The owner already selected defeated enemy cohorts and completed-wave recovery. Those are inherited requirements; no comparison attempts to reopen them. The meaningful engineering choice is how to commit checkpoint/reward state.

Selected recommendation: strong, one versioned save envelope. Its bounded record and existing verified-write seam support stable per-wave commits. Separate checkpoint and reward stores with a recovery journal are viable but add transaction states and mismatch recovery without a current scale benefit. Local single-tab storage remains the same trust boundary. No new package/service is needed.

Catalogs use stable IDs for tiers, milestone reward types and fitting capabilities. This directly addresses the owner request to scale past two options. Data validation permits only known grant types and bounded values; local data never becomes server-authoritative rewards.

ScalableStrong: bounded checkpoint and milestone ledger grow with authored content, not run duration.Adequate: content growth fits, but each store adds recovery coordination.
MaintainableStrong: one versioned parser, migration and confirmed-write owner.Weak: journal, checkpoint and reward parsers must evolve together.
AvailableStrong: failure preserves the last complete record and existing retry path.Adequate: recovery works after reconstructing incomplete multi-store writes.

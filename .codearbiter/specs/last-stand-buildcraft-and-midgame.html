Last Stand — Buildcraft, mid-game campaign and playtest readiness

Approved sprint: two-bay builds and saved loadouts, new fittings/research, six-tier authored campaign, earned balance and actual candidate qualification. Includes integration and merges of the existing PR stack and applicable dependency work after required checks.

Intent

INT-BUILDCRAFT

Intent

CallerLast Stand owner and returning farming players
OutcomePlayers can earn and compare distinct builds, pursue six authored tiers without abandoning profitable lower-tier farms, and trust that progress and readable controls survive longer sessions and restarts.
ProblemThe integrated game has shared foundations but only one auxiliary at a time, four economy/speed research tracks and four tiers. It needs interacting build choices and meaningful mid-game goals, while actual platform and long-run release proof still lags implementation.

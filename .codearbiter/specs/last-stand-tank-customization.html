Sections

DESIGN

Player workflow

Blocks

Open Appearance from the garage menu. Preview a named kit or choose Mobility, Hull, Turret, Barrel and Paint independently. Actual tank stays visible with rotation and selected-part highlight. Apply saves; Cancel/Escape/exit restores the saved tank. No appearance edits during active combat. Distinguish cosmetic Railgun style from gameplay Railgun fitting.

SMARTS

Choices and limits

Blocks

Persistent rig plus replaceable visual children beats 256 prefabs on maintainability, scalability and testability. Existing profile metadata beats another storage key on portability and concurrency. Grouped named choices beat cycle-only discovery. A genuine Apply/Cancel draft deliberately adapts the legacy immediate-edit workflow for comparing 3D assemblies. New versioned art preserves original sources. Mesh targets are authoring limits, not device performance proof. Imagegen concept is art reference, not engine output or acceptance evidence.

SEC-SMARTS

Design rationale

Blocks

The owner selected consolidation of existing owners. Explicit modules and a small page interface preserve scalable catalog-driven screens, improve maintainability and direct testability, retain availability through current offline/save behavior, preserve reliable transactions and native ownership checks, and introduce no new security boundary. No competing architecture is needed for this scoped change.

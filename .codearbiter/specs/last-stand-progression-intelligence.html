SEC-DESIGN

Design and tradeoffs

Blocks

A read-only progression planner composes existing catalogs and snapshots; existing Shop and Lab remain sole action owners. Advisory pin storage belongs in the existing preferences owner, not the progression ledger. Exact history cohorts are intentionally conservative because stored contexts are opaque and old saves do not contain typed stat vectors. This sacrifices sample volume for honest comparisons. No projected return-on-investment or time-to-afford promise is made from sparse history.

References: SRC-OWNERS
SEC-BOUNDARIES

Delivery and qualification

Blocks

This is an implementation sprint, not platform release approval. Prior unresolved human/device/listening/10-hour qualification remains open in its owning plan. No historical RED receipt is recreated, and no frozen run edition or retained evidence is edited. No automatic purchases, prompts, cloud save, balance changes, merge or publication.

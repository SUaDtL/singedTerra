CR-SPEED

Half-step game speed with truthful research limits

must
Applicability
Modeunconditional
RationalePart of the revised player-controls sprint; the optional payoff cue alone is conditional on observed value.
Constraint refs
  • CON-APPROVAL
  • CON-PRESERVE
  • CON-EVIDENCE
Guarantees
  • Initial available values are 0, 0.5 and 1; default positive rate is 1. Plus stops at the current 1x cap and explains that higher speeds belong to future research.
  • Pause/Resume and the speed readout use the same owner. Minus from 0.5 selects 0; plus from 0 selects 0.5; Resume restores the last positive rate.
  • Admit wall-time segments at their active rate into the existing fixed .05-second simulation clock. Preserve fractional carry, callback budget, terminal horizon and retained debt; changing rate never rescales earlier elapsed time.
  • Settle the action boundary before a rate change; reject/disable conflicting changes during catch-up rather than retroactively rescaling backlog. Menu pause, manual pause and focus transitions remain consistent.

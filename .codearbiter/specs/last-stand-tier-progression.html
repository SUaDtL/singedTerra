Last Stand: Tier Goals and Personal Bests

Give the existing upgrade loop a persistent purpose: beat a personal record and unlock a harder tier.

Criteria

CR-TIER

Tier selection and immutable admission

must
Applicability
Modeunconditional
RationaleRequired by approved slice.
Constraint refs
  • CON-BOUNDARY
Guarantees
  • Tier 1 is initially available with a 1200-tick (60-second) goal and current encounter rules. A saved qualifying Tier 1 terminal run unlocks Tier 2 with an 1800-tick (90-second) goal. Tier 2 changes only enemy initial and maximum hull to ceil(base * 5/4). Locked tiers refuse deployment; admission binds the tier and profile version immutably for that run. Both tiers remain selectable after unlock.

CR-OUTCOME

Terminal outcomes and manual rewards

must
Applicability
Modeunconditional
RationaleRequired by approved slice.
Constraint refs
  • CON-BOUNDARY
Guarantees
  • Ordinary defeat stages exactly one Salvage for manual exactly-once Claim. The existing 3600-tick (180-second) safety horizon may finalize qualifying records and unlocks but pays zero and is labeled a non-victory limit result. Unfinished leave/reload grants no records, unlock or award. Pending results resolve before deploy. A defeat on the exact goal tick qualifies after that combat tick resolves.

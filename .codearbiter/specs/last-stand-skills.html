DEC-TUNING

Initial roster and earned progression tuning

Authorityproposed
ChoiceInitial IDs/keys: Overdrive/overdrive, EmergencyRepairs/emergencyRepairs, Minefield/minefield, ReinforcedHull/reinforcedHull, Ballistics/ballistics, ReactiveLining/reactiveLining. Three ranks use cumulative copies 1/3/6. Tier 1 best wave 25 makes Overdrive and Reinforced Hull eligible; wave 100 adds Emergency Repairs and Ballistics; wave 300 adds Minefield and Reactive Lining. Shared slots unlock at Tier 1 best waves 25/100/500 with capacities 1/2/3. Each draw costs one Skill Intel and selects uniformly among eligible unfinished skills. Earn one Intel on each hundredth cleared wave in any tier, repeatably, and once at Tier 1 waves 25 and 50 using a durable award ledger. No retroactive rewards on migration. Overdrive uses ceil(base interval * 2/3) for 12/15/18 simulation seconds. Emergency Repairs restores 30/40/50 percent of maximum hull and refuses full hull. Minefield arms one ring of radius 10000; entry triggers one hit to all enemies in range for 4/5/6 times captured cannon damage through normal shield/armor handling. Reinforced Hull increases maximum hull by 10/15/20 percent; Ballistics increases weapon damage by 8/12/16 percent; Reactive Lining reduces incoming damage after flat armor by 10/15/20 percent with minimum one damage. No recharge, paid draws, equipment itemization, targeting priority progression or away rewards are added by this sprint.
RationaleExplicit agent-selected initial values under the authorized sprint provide a bounded complete system to measure and tune. They do not claim historic owner selection of these numbers.
Source refSRC-TUNING
DEC-LIFECYCLE

Captured skill state and atomic recovery

Authorityproposed
ChoiceProfile v8 migrates to v9 with empty skill ownership, no Intel, and no fabricated award history. Old run editions capture neutral skills. A new run captures skill IDs/ranks and one use per equipped active. Skill effects and timers use simulation time; menus/pause freeze them, closed time does not advance them. The existing completed-wave checkpoint boundary remains atomic: on interruption, the whole unfinished wave rolls back including skill uses/effects; confirmed skill use is retained with the confirmed combat result. Draw selection occurs once before the existing pending Commit; retry preserves the same outcome and debit. All three existing named presets include fittings and skills atomically. Collection development and preparation edits do not alter a captured run.
RationalePreserve the current wave transaction and replay boundary; restore combat effects and resource uses consistently rather than inventing a second skill save or refilling uses independently.

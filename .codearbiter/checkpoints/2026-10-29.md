### Security reviewer

#### SEC-M1 — stale roster overwrite

- **Severity / disposition:** MEDIUM / DEFERRABLE.
- **Location:** `supabase/functions/update_player/index.ts:139`.
- **Finding:** An id-only PATCH replaces a roster derived from an earlier snapshot. Concurrent start/ready/join/leave/edit operations can be overwritten; a transport fixture reproduced ready state being reset after activation.

- **Control:** Input validation and bounded request failure.
